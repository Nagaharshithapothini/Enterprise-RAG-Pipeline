Enterprise AI Platform Overview

The enterprise AI platform provides document intelligence,
semantic search, and knowledge discovery capabilities for
internal business users.

The platform uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to retrieve
relevant enterprise information before generating responses.

Documents are processed through ingestion, text cleaning,
chunking, embedding generation, vector indexing, retrieval,
reranking, and response-generation stages.

Semantic embeddings are stored in a vector database.
User questions are converted into embeddings and compared
against stored document vectors to identify relevant content.

A reranking model is applied after initial vector retrieval.
The reranker evaluates the relationship between the user's
question and retrieved document chunks and prioritizes the
most relevant information.

The language model receives only the highest-ranking document
context. Responses are instructed to remain grounded in the
retrieved information and provide source references.

The architecture includes safeguards designed to reduce
hallucinations. If retrieved information does not adequately
answer a question, the assistant should indicate that the
information is unavailable rather than generating unsupported
content.

The platform exposes document-ingestion and question-answering
capabilities through REST APIs.

Production deployments can include authentication,
role-based access control, logging, observability,
evaluation, model monitoring, and document-level access
controls.
